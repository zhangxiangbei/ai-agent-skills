# Community Discussion Skill

## Purpose

Turn a research judgment into a discussion prompt for AI Evolution Island or other learning communities.

## Workflow

1. Summarize the topic in one sentence.
2. Explain why ordinary people should care.
3. Ask 2-3 open questions.
4. Provide one action-oriented reflection prompt.
5. Avoid pressure, hype, or information overload.

## Output Format

- Topic
- Why it matters
- Discussion questions
- Action reflection
- Optional reading link

## Safety

Use public-safe examples only. Do not include private member information or internal community operations.

# Review Rubric

## 1. Core Judgment

Is the main judgment clear, specific, and useful?

## 2. Cognitive Value

Does the content reduce cognitive cost for ordinary readers?

## 3. Evidence Awareness

Are facts, examples, and judgments clearly separated?

## 4. Structure

Is the structure easy to follow?

## 5. Tone

Is the tone restrained, research-oriented, and readable?

## 6. Safety

Does the content avoid private information, fake data, investment advice, and unsupported claims?

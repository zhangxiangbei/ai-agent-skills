# Xiaohongshu Note Skill

## Purpose

Convert a research judgment into a Xiaohongshu-style note while preserving the judgment quality of Zhang Xiangbei AI Evolution.

## Workflow

1. Extract one useful judgment.
2. Write a clear title without exaggerated claims.
3. Create a short opening that states the problem.
4. Use 3-5 short sections.
5. End with a practical takeaway.

## Style

- Short paragraphs
- Strong but restrained title
- Practical and readable
- No fake numbers
- No unsupported trend claims

## Safety

Do not include account credentials, personal private information, or platform manipulation tactics.

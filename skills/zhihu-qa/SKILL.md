# Zhihu Q&A Skill

## Purpose

Transform a research judgment into a Zhihu-style Q&A answer for ordinary readers.

## Workflow

1. Restate the question in plain language.
2. Give the core answer early.
3. Explain the reasoning in 3-5 sections.
4. Use examples only when they are public-safe and non-sensitive.
5. End with a practical takeaway.

## Style

- Clear and analytical
- Not clickbait
- Not overly promotional
- Avoid unsupported real-time data

## Safety

Do not include private information, accounts, backend paths, or unverifiable company numbers.

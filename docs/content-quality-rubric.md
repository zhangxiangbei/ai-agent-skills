# Content Quality Rubric

## Core Standard

内容必须降低用户认知成本，提高决策质量。

## Review Dimensions

1. Core judgment clarity
2. Fact and judgment separation
3. Ordinary reader understandability
4. Evidence and source awareness
5. Structure and readability
6. Platform fit
7. Tone consistency
8. Safety and privacy

## Scoring

Use a 1-5 score for each dimension.

- 1: Not publishable
- 2: Needs major rewrite
- 3: Structurally usable, needs revision
- 4: Publishable after light edit
- 5: Strong and reusable

## Red Flags

- Unsupported real-time data
- Overstated claims
- Fake citations
- Investment advice language
- Private account or token exposure
- Platform-specific manipulation tactics
